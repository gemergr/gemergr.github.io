Para Play Juegos y Google Play rige la relación que tienes directamente con Google.

No tenemos servidores ni bases de datos propias. Tu progreso vive en tu dispositivo, y lo que va a Google viaja cifrado. Las cuentas con que administramos el juego (Google Play Console y Firebase) están protegidas con verificación en dos pasos, y solo el representante legal tiene acceso.

What data we process and why

Game progress

Levels, stars, scores, items and purchases already delivered. Stored only on your device; we don't receive it.

Purpose
So the game remembers your progress.
Legal basis
Necessary to provide the game you downloaded.
Retention
Until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Google Play Games

If you use Play Games, Google gives us your player ID and player name. They are used to show your scores on leaderboards, rank you in the weekly Cup, and save your progress in Google's cloud (saved games). You can play without Play Games.

Purpose
Leaderboards, the weekly Cup and cloud saves.
Legal basis
Necessary to provide those features when you use them.
Retention
As long as you keep your Play Games profile. You manage and delete it in the Google Play Games app or your Google account.

Google also processes this data as an independent controller under its privacy policy.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play. We only receive the purchase receipt (order number and product), never your payment details.

Purpose
Deliver what you bought, restore it if you reinstall, and meet the company's accounting and tax obligations.
Legal basis
Your purchase and the company's legal obligations.
Retention
The receipt stays on your device. Google Play sales reports are kept for the period required by tax law (up to 6 years).

Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, by Google. They receive an app installation identifier (not your name or email), device model, system and game versions, approximate country, game events (for example, starting or finishing a level, or making a purchase) and crash reports.

Purpose
Tune difficulty, find problem levels and fix bugs. Not used for advertising.
Legal basis
Our legitimate interest in keeping the game stable and well balanced. You can object (see Your rights).
Retention
Data linked to an identifier is deleted after 2 months (analytics) and 90 days (crash reports). Only aggregate figures that identify no one remain.

Internet time

The game asks a Google server for the current time so the level calendar and daily rewards work correctly. It sends no data about you; Google only sees the IP address, as with any connection.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your address and what you tell us only to reply. Legal basis: your own request. We keep them for up to 2 years after resolving it.

Where the data comes from

From your use of the game, and from Google when you use Play Games or make a purchase. We don't use publicly available sources.

Who we share it with

Only Google, which provides the services above: Firebase processes data on our behalf, and Play Games and Google Play process it under Google's policy. We don't sell, rent or transfer data to anyone else, and we don't share it with advertisers.

International transfers

Google's servers are outside Chile, mainly in the United States. For Firebase, the transfer is governed by the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms, which require Google to process the data only on our instructions and to protect it. For Play Games and Google Play, your own relationship with Google applies.

Security

We have no servers or databases of our own. Your progress lives on your device, and what goes to Google is encrypted in transit. The accounts we use to run the game (Google Play Console and Firebase) are protected with 2-step verification, and only the legal representative has access.

Your rights

You can ask, free of charge, for:

  • Access: to know whether we process your data and get a copy.
  • Rectification: to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: to have your data deleted.
  • Objection: to stop a specific use, such as analytics.
  • Portability: to receive your data in a common electronic format.
  • Blocking: to suspend its use while we resolve another request of yours.

How: email gemergr11@gmail.com saying which right you want to exercise. To find your data, tell us your Play Games player name or the identifier we ask for.

Deadlines: we acknowledge and answer within 30 calendar days, extendable once by another 30. Blocking requests are answered within 2 business days.

If we don't answer or we reject your request, you can file a complaint with Chile's Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales) within 30 business days.

To delete your data without contacting us: uninstall the game to delete the progress on your device, and delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google account.

Automated decisions

We make no automated decisions that have legal effects on you or significantly affect you. The weekly Cup only ranks game scores.

Children

Brimful is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.
